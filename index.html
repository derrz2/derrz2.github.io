BRAIN SCIENCE × MACHINE LEARNING

Wenhao Ye

I study how to learn meaningful representations of the human brain, with the goal of bridging the gap between brain modeling and real-world applications through AI.

I am an undergraduate in Biomedical Engineering at Shenzhen University, and a visiting research student at the Neural Computing and Control (NCC) Lab, SUSTech, advised by Prof. Quanying Liu. I also have the opportunity to collaborate with Prof. Hongkai Wen and Prof. Jianfeng Feng at Fudan University and the University of Warwick.

My research focuses on NeuroAI, particularly foundation models and representation learning for fMRI. I am also exploring trustworthy AI systems with JEPA and uncertainty-aware learning.
